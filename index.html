Overview

Healing is possible. It begins with a desire to step into the dark and walk through the unknown.

When it feels impossible

It’s hard to imagine healing is even possible when we are deep in betrayal trauma, looping experiences, and PTSD symptoms. Betrayal of every kind has a massive impact on us emotionally and mentally, not to mention the toll it takes on our bodies and spirit.

As a couple who has been down this path, we know from personal experience that you will come out of whatever your situation is an empowered, awakened individual, more connected to yourself, your life, and your relationships than you thought possible.

How do we heal?

The core of all healing is changing our perceptions of ourselves and disidentifying with the story, which is usually built around victimhood. We have to be willing to think differently about ourselves and the other in order to fully find inner peace.

Healing is not only possible, it’s the path the Universe desires to lead us through as a fundamental part of our human experience.

What this program does

Find the inner story beneath infidelity

The intention of this course is to help you identify what infidelity is really about, and the inner story beneath it, so a new belief pattern and understanding can develop. Infidelity leaves a huge imprint emotionally, energetically, physically, and spiritually. Understanding its roots can help you move through this potent healing entirely transformed.

Couples and individuals in this program are given the tools to take their power back: rejecting victim mentality, stepping into their sovereignty, forgiving the other and the self, and building a new agreement for the relationship.

We actively work on an awakened perspective on intimate relationships ourselves, and we teach from the wisdom of our lived experience.

Our story

An overview of our healing journey

As of September 5, 2024, we’ve been married for 15 years. We are parents to four children and are so incredibly in love. But our marriage has been anything but easy.

Over the course of our relationship, betrayal was more than a one-time occurrence. We know betrayal can come in various forms, because we’ve lived it.

In spring 2018 we hit an all-time low and finally saw that we needed to give maximum priority to our individual healing. We began couples therapy and individual therapy, the beginning of a deep dive into our emotional health. The pattern of behavior had to be fully and consciously confronted in order to be healed.

From 2020 onward, healing has been our way of life. We’ve been on a journey to free ourselves from our own enslavement, and this course is a complete distillation of our lived wisdom. We have completely transformed our relationship with each other, and most importantly, our relationship with our own selves.

Infidelity is a rampant and widespread problem in modern society, yet it is still judged and taboo to talk about. Our intention is to turn that upside down, so couples can turn these deep wounds into the gateway of their freedom.

Program FAQ

How long is the program?

Each block is 4 weeks: three weeks of teaching, one module per week, and a fourth week for integration and a coaching call. You can move through the program one block at a time, continuing when it feels right, with a 2 to 4 week break between blocks.

If you take all three blocks, that’s 12 weeks of sessions, usually completed within 4 to 6 months.

Is this a recorded or live course?

This is a live online course. You’ll meet virtually with Britton and Carolee and the others registered for your block. Sessions are recorded for your personal review only.

Can I do the program if I’m no longer partnered or have a new partner?

Yes. This program isn’t designed just for couples. It is designed to re-empower anyone who has undergone the experience of betrayal, whether you were the one in violation or the one violated. It is equally useful and impactful to both sides of a betrayal experience.

Do my partner and I need to do the program together?

We understand that not everyone is ready to take on this type of deep healing work, and not every relationship is at the same vibration. This program is designed to heal individuals and relationships.

While not required, if you’re still partnered we highly recommend the two of you take the program together. It is much more effective to have both of you doing the healing work at the same time.

What payment options are available?

We take payment through Venmo, PayPal, or bank wire. You can pay in full, or use our 3-installment payment plan.

How much time commitment should I expect?

We get it, you might feel busy. Truth is, if you honestly knew the feeling of being whole again (which you’ve actually only forgotten), you wouldn’t be asking about the time commitment, yet here we are.

Sessions last around 180 minutes, and there are integration practices to do between sessions. A good estimate is 4 to 6 hours per module week.
